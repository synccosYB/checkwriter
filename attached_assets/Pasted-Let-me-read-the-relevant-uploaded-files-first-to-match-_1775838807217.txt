Key design decisions worth noting:

The endpoint responds with 202 Accepted immediately, then fires the send loop asynchronously — no timeout risk even for large user bases
Emails are batched in chunks of 10 with a 200ms delay between chunks to stay within SendGrid rate limits
Every send attempt (success or failure) is logged to admin_messages with isMassEmail: true, so per-user email history from ticket #26 automatically shows mass emails with a distinct badge
The modal is a true 3-step flow: Compose → Confirm (with recipient count) → Success — the confirmation step satisfies the ticket requirement of showing "how many users will receive the email before sending"
