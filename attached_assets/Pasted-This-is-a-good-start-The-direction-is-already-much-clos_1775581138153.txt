This is a good start. The direction is already much closer to the new brand than the old Bootstrap look.

What’s working:

The split-screen layout feels more premium.
The navy left panel already gives a stronger fintech tone.
The login form is cleaner and more focused.
The stats row on the left helps the page feel more product-led.

What still needs refinement to fully match #23:

Biggest issues

The right form panel feels too bare and slightly unfinished compared to the left side. The left side looks branded, but the right side still feels like a basic form placed on a blank canvas.

Specific feedback for developer
1. Right panel needs a real auth card

Right now the form is floating on a white background.
Make it a contained auth card or at least a structured form panel with:

subtle shadow
soft radius
tighter internal spacing
max width around 420–460px
slightly stronger vertical rhythm

That alone will make it feel much more like a premium product login.

2. Heading hierarchy needs improvement

“Login” is too generic and visually lonely.

Use:

Title: Welcome back
Subtitle: something like
Sign in to manage checks, mailing, and financial workflows securely.

That gives the page warmth and clarity.

3. Form spacing is uneven

The fields, checkbox row, social login, signup link, and demo CTA feel stacked without strong grouping.

Break the form into clear groups:

heading/subtext
email/password
primary CTA
remember/forgot row
divider
social login
signup/demo section

Each group should have intentional spacing.

4. Buttons need stronger hierarchy

Right now:

Submit is okay
Google button is acceptable
Demo button feels disconnected

Recommended hierarchy:

Primary button: full-width navy filled
Google button: full-width white outlined
Demo button: tertiary/subtle outlined or soft tint button

Also change Submit to Sign in.

5. Checkbox + forgot password row needs alignment

The “Remember Me” and “Forget Password?” area feels awkward and crowded.

Fix:

keep both on one horizontal row
left align checkbox + label
right align forgot password link
vertically center them

Also rename to Forgot password?

6. Input styling should feel more premium

The inputs are clean, but still a little generic.

Improve with:

slightly taller field height
stronger focus state
softer border color
more refined label spacing
better password icon alignment
7. Left panel content should move slightly upward

The left panel content sits a little too low and isolated.

Try:

centering it vertically a bit higher
tightening the gap between icon, headline, paragraph, and stats
making the icon area feel more branded, possibly with a cleaner mark container
8. Left panel copy can be tightened

Current copy is decent, but it can sound sharper.

Example:
Professional Check Management
Securely create, print, mail, and manage checks from one professional platform.

That reads a bit more direct.

9. Stats row is good, but could look more refined

The stats are useful, but the typography can be improved:

larger number
smaller muted label
even spacing between the 3 items
maybe very subtle divider lines between them
10. Bottom legal/support area is missing

A premium auth page usually has a small footer area under the form for:

Terms
Privacy
maybe support/help text

That would also help the page feel complete.

Overall verdict

This is not bad at all.
It already looks like a valid first-pass redesign.

But for #23, I would tell the developer:

“Good foundation, but the right side still feels too plain. Push it further into a true premium auth experience with a contained form card, stronger heading/subtitle hierarchy, better grouped spacing, cleaner action hierarchy, and more polished alignment.”
